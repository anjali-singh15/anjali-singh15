## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧪 01. LabTriage AI — Intelligent Laboratory Report Analysis

An AI-powered project focused on simplifying laboratory report analysis through document processing, information extraction, and intelligent insights. The project explores how AI and backend technologies can help transform complex medical laboratory documents into structured, easier-to-understand information.

**✨ Key Areas**
- 📄 **Document Processing:** Extracting relevant information from uploaded reports and documents.
- 🔍 **Information Extraction:** Identifying important details from unstructured document content.
- 🧠 **AI-Assisted Analysis:** Exploring language models and semantic search for understanding extracted information.
- 🖼️ **OCR & Image Processing:** Working with scanned documents and image-based reports.
- 📊 **Structured Results:** Organizing extracted information into a format suitable for review and interpretation.
- 🔐 **Backend & Data Management:** Building APIs and managing application data.

**Tech Stack**

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `React` · `PostgreSQL` · `LangChain` · `Vector Search` · `OCR` · `AI Model Integration` · `Docker`



### 📊 02. PulseX — Customer 360° Digital Twin

A customer intelligence platform built for the CRM-04 hackathon challenge. PulseX brings customer data and business signals together to help teams understand customer health and identify retention opportunities.

**Key Features**

- 👤 **Customer 360°:** Consolidated customer profiles and interaction timelines.
- 📈 **Customer Insights:** Visualizations for customer activity, purchases, complaints, and payments.
- 💬 **Sentiment Analysis:** Rule-based sentiment insights from customer interactions.
- ⚠️ **Risk Intelligence:** Explainable risk indicators and churn-risk estimates.
- 🔗 **Identity Resolution:** Matching customer records across different data sources.
- 🔄 **Event Simulation:** Simulating customer events and customer-status changes.
- 🎯 **Retention Opportunities:** Insights to help identify customers who may need attention.

**Tech Stack**

`React` · `Vite` · `React Router` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Recharts` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `MongoDB` · `Mongoose` · `Server-Sent Events`

**Approach:** Churn-risk estimates use transparent, rule-based logic rather than a trained predictive ML model.

🔗 **[View PulseX on GitHub](https://github.com/anjali-singh15/PulseX)**

### ⛓️ 03. Blockchain-Based Fake Product Review Detection System

A web application exploring product-review authenticity through order verification, suspicious-activity detection, and blockchain-based record integrity.

**Key Features**

- 🛒 **Order-Based Verification:** Review-submission checks using order details and registered customer information.
- 🛡️ **Fraud Detection:** Rule-based checks for duplicate submissions, rapid review activity, and suspicious similarities.
- 📊 **Fraud Scoring:** A rule-based score to flag potentially suspicious reviews.
- ⛓️ **Blockchain Integrity:** Smart-contract integration to record cryptographic hashes of genuine reviews.
- 🧑‍💼 **Admin Dashboard:** Review-management workflows and a queue for reviewing flagged submissions.

**Tech Stack**

`Solidity 0.8.24` · `Hardhat` · `React 18` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `MongoDB Atlas` · `Tailwind CSS` · `JWT Authentication`

🔗 **[View Blockchain Project on GitHub](https://github.com/anjali-singh15/blockchain-review-system)**
